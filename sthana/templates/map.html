Spatial reasoning.

Each seat is a node. Neighbouring seats are joined by edges. A graph neural network passes messages along them to refine every seat's estimate.

  1. Nodes. Every tracked seat, with the detector's observation and how confident it was.
  2. Edges. Each seat links to its nearest seats, measured in desk sizes so camera perspective doesn't distort distance.
  3. Messages. Two rounds of message passing blend each seat's own evidence and history with its neighbours'. Busy areas cluster.
  4. Gated by confidence. A clear detection barely moves. An uncertain or hidden seat leans on context, so a person walking past a camera doesn't blank out a desk.

Uses: occluded seats stay on the map as inferred, weak detections are flagged uncertain, and the best-seat pick favours quiet neighbourhoods. It never overrides a confident detection.
